About DhiAI Academy

The intellect behind the intelligence.

Dhī (धी) is the Sanskrit word for intellect: the faculty that understands, discerns and decides. AI is the most powerful tool that faculty has ever been handed. DhiAI Academy exists because the second should never replace the first.

धी

Dhī + AI

Intellect and intelligence: the human mind that decides, and the machine that helps it.

Our mark

Two lines, one point.

The curved line in our symbol is human thinking: intuitive and adaptive. The straight line is machine intelligence: precise and consistent. They rise towards each other and meet at a single point above them, the moment of insight that neither reaches alone.

Read together, the form is also a capital A, for Academy and for AI.

What we believe

Four ideas behind every programme.

01

Learn about AI, with AI, through AI. Then build.

Understanding how AI works, using it as a partner, learning your own subjects through it, and building with it are four different skills. We teach all four.

02

AI is for every student.

Not only future engineers. A commerce or humanities student needs to question AI as much as a computer science student does.

03

The human stays in charge.

Judgement, ethics and curiosity are not outsourced. Students decide what AI may do, and check what it says.

04

Practical beats theoretical.

Every programme ends with something a student built and can show, and explain line by line.

Vision

A generation that understands, builds and responsibly uses AI for the greater good.

Mission

Bring practical, applied AI learning into every student's education, moving them from consuming technology to understanding, testing, creating and applying it.

What we are

  • An applied-AI academy that starts with school students.
  • Built around human judgement, with real work at the end of every programme.
  • Rooted in Indian thought, fluent in modern technology.

What we are not

  • A coding institute.
  • A tuition centre.
  • A course on how to click through AI tools.
  • A children's app.
