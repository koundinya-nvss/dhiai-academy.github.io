Questions

What parents and schools ask us.

Q01Does my child need to know coding?

No, not for Beginner. Python comes in on Day 5, starting from installing it and running a first script.

Advanced expects students to have finished Beginner, or to be able to run a Python script already.

Q02Which classes and boards is it for?

Classes 10 to 12, from any board (CBSE, ICSE/ISC, Cambridge IGCSE/A Level, IB) and any stream. We build on what each board already teaches instead of repeating it.

Q03How is it scheduled?

Each programme has five teaching days and one buffer day, spread over six weekends. Beginner days are 2 hours long; Advanced days are 4 hours. Dates are set for each cohort.

Q04What does my child need?

A laptop they can install software on (Windows or macOS works best), a Google account set up with a parent's consent, and their notes for one chapter of a subject they are studying. Before Day 1 we share what to install and how.

Q05Which AI tools are used, and is it safe?

Mainly Google's Gemini, plus Python, VS Code and Jupyter.

Privacy is taught from the first day: what never to paste into an AI tool, what connected apps can see, and how to disconnect them. Projects use students' own study notes or sample data, so nothing personal or sensitive is needed.

Q06Won't students just use AI to do their homework?

That is exactly the habit we work against. Students set AI up as a tutor that quizzes them, check its answers against their sources, and learn to credit AI the way they would credit any source. The goal is sharper thinking, not less of it.

Q07Is there homework?

No required homework. The project is built and finished during class hours. Each day has an optional "Try it at home" exercise for students who want to go further.

Q08What if my child misses a day?

The sixth day is a buffer: anyone who is behind catches up before Demo Day. If you know about a clash in advance, tell us early.

Q09Does it count towards school marks?

Some board AI subjects give credit for AI training, bootcamps and projects. Whether our programme counts is your school's decision. We are happy to share exactly what we cover.

Q10How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the programme and on whether it runs through a school. Send us an enquiry and we'll share the details.

Q11Can our school host a cohort?

Yes. See For schools for how it works, or book a conversation through the enquiry form.
