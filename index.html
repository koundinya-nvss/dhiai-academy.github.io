Why we exist

Students already use AI every day. Few are taught to use it well.

Most school syllabi teach AI as theory: definitions, project cycles, the vocabulary of ethics. Very few have students use generative AI hands-on, check it against sources, or build something real with it.

That gap is where habits form. A student who learns to copy from AI gets lazier. A student who learns to question it, test it and build with it gets sharper. We teach the second way.

What we believe

Learn about AI, with AI, through AI. Then build.

Dhī is the intellect that understands, discerns and decides. AI is the most powerful tool it has ever been handed. Our job is to make sure the tool never replaces the thinker.

Learn about AI

How it actually works, and where it fails. Students see why a model can sound certain and still be wrong.

Learn with AI

AI as a tutor, not a ghost-writer. Students set it up to quiz them and make them explain, so the thinking stays theirs.

Learn through AI

Their own subjects, with every answer checked. Students ground AI in sources they trust and verify the claims that matter.

Then build

Every programme ends with a working project the student designed, built and can explain, shown at Demo Day.

From AI user to AI thinker

  1. UseAsk precise questions and read the answers critically.
  2. QuestionCheck AI against evidence and spot confident mistakes.
  3. BuildTurn AI into a tool for a real problem in their own life.
  4. ThinkDecide what AI should and shouldn't do, and explain why.

How a day works

Explain it. Show it live. Build it together.

01 · Concept

One idea, explained

In plain language, with the technical term named and explained the first time.

02 · Live demo

Shown on screen

The idea working for real in Gemini or Python, including the moments it goes wrong.

03 · Hands-on

Built together

Students build the day's piece on their own laptops, step by step with the instructor.

04 · Optional

Try it at home

One short exercise to stretch the day's skill. Never needed to finish the project.

Demo Day
