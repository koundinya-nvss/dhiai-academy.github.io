Responsible by design

Safe, honest use of AI is part of the syllabus.

We teach students under 18, so we treat privacy and integrity as skills to practise, not rules to recite.

  • Students use AI through their own Google accounts, set up with a parent's consent.
  • From Day 1, students learn what never to paste into an AI tool, and how to see and remove what connected apps can access.
  • Projects use students' own study notes or sample data. Nothing personal or sensitive is needed.
  • The Advanced app runs on the student's laptop and needs no API key.
  • Students learn to disclose and credit AI help, the way they credit any source.
